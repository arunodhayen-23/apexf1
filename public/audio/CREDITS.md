# Engine audio credits

The game includes these recorded Formula One engine sounds by Ed Pond (Wikimedia Commons contributor name: Edvvc):

- **McLaren-Mercedes MP4/23 (2008)** — recorded at the 2010 Goodwood Festival of Speed. This is the V8 sound. [Original recording](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:McLaren-Mercedes_MP4_23_(2008).ogg)
- **Red Bull-Cosworth RB1 (2005)** — recorded at the 2010 Goodwood Festival of Speed. This is the V10 sound. [Original recording](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_Bull-Cosworth_RB1_(2005).ogg)

Both recordings are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The audio files are included unmodified; the game loops them and adjusts playback speed during the race to follow the selected engine's revs.
